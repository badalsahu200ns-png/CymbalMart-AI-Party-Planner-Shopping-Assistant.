Alexa+ Companion Dialogue

Text + Voice
CymbalMart Alexa+ Assistant Hi! I'm your CymbalMart Alexa+ party shopping companion. I can plan celebrations, adjust shopping item quantities, and keep your budget balanced. Try saying: “Plan a birthday party for 12 people with a budget of 5000 rupees”.
Microphone support utilizes the browser Web Speech API with fallback to text input. Audio speech feedback is provided via Web Speech Synthesis.
Transparency Note: This is an independent web prototype developed for the Amazon Developer Hackathon (Alexa+ Track). It simulates the conversational shopping experience and does not connect to a production Alexa+ hardware device or official Amazon Alexa Skill MCP endpoint.
